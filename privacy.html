Privacy Policy

How this site handles your information

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Instagram Post Poster Generator does and does not collect, and how third-party services used on this site handle data. We have designed the tool to be privacy friendly: it runs almost entirely inside your own browser.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

The short version

This is a static website. We do not operate a server-side database, we do not require an account, and we do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. The links you paste and the posters you generate stay in your browser.

Information we collect

The site itself collects no server-side personal data. There is no login, no registration form, and no backend that records the Instagram URLs you enter. When you paste a post link and generate a poster, that activity happens locally in your browser and is not transmitted to any server that we operate.

Local, in-browser processing

The poster download feature uses your browser's built-in Screen Capture API (getDisplayMedia). When you choose to download a poster, your browser asks for permission to capture the current tab and the resulting image is created and saved entirely on your own device. The captured image is not uploaded anywhere and is never sent to us or to any third party.

Third-party embeds and cookies

Instagram embeds

To display a post, the tool loads Instagram's official embed directly from instagram.com. Because that content is served by Instagram (Meta), Instagram may set its own cookies and collect data according to its own policies when the embed loads. This is outside our control. You can review how Meta handles this in the Instagram Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

We may display advertising through Google AdSense on some pages of this site once ads are enabled. When that happens, Google and its partners may use cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your browsing activity.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google's Advertising Policies, and opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices.

Note: at the time of the last update above, the content and support pages of this site (Home, About, Contact, and this Privacy Policy) do not carry advertising.

Your rights and choices

  • You can use the tool without providing any personal information to us.
  • You can manage or block cookies at any time through your browser settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising using the Google and industry links above.
  • You can decline the screen capture permission prompt; the tool will simply not download a poster.

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no account information for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. If you have questions about your rights, please reach out using the contact details below.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the site or to legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how the site works, please visit our Contact page to reach us by email.
