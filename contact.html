Contact Us

We would love to hear from you

Get in touch

Email is the best way to reach us. Drop us a message at contact@example.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

This is a lightweight, static website with no sign-up and no contact form to fill out, so email keeps things simple and direct.

What to contact us about

  • Bug reports. If a post will not load, the QR code looks wrong, or the poster download fails, let us know the Instagram URL you tried and the browser you are using so we can reproduce the problem.
  • Feedback and ideas. Have a suggestion for a new feature or an improvement to the layout? We read every message and use it to guide what we build next.
  • Privacy requests. For anything relating to your privacy or the handling of data, email us and see our Privacy Policy for full details on how the site works.

Please note that we are not affiliated with Instagram or Meta and cannot help with account issues, password resets, or content disputes on Instagram itself.
